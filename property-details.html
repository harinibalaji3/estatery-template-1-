Property Overview

Skyline Residency is a thoughtfully designed 3BHK premium apartment located in the heart of Anna Nagar, Chennai. The property combines contemporary architecture, spacious interiors and convenient access to schools, shopping, healthcare and public transportation.

Located on the 9th floor, this bright corner apartment provides excellent cross ventilation, natural lighting, a modern modular kitchen and two covered parking spaces. It is an ideal choice for families looking for comfort, connectivity and a modern urban lifestyle.
