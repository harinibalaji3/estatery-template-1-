OUR STORY

Making real estate easier for everyone

Estatery was founded in 2018 with a simple idea: finding the right property should not feel complicated.

We created a platform where people can discover properties, compare options, connect with trusted agents and make decisions using clear and useful information.

From a small beginning to a growing real estate platform, our focus has remained the same — putting people first and making every property journey more transparent.
